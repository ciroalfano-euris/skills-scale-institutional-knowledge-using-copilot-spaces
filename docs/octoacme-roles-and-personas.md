# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Architects shape the solution design, technical standards, and integration approach for the project. They ensure the team can deliver a scalable, maintainable, and secure implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Define solution architecture and technical guardrails
- Review designs for scalability, performance, and maintainability
- Guide integration decisions and dependency management
- Partner with Product and Engineering leads on trade-offs and feasibility
- Help identify technical risks and long-term platform impacts

### Goals
- Support delivery with a clear technical direction
- Reduce rework caused by architectural drift
- Improve system quality, scalability, and operability

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and design discussions
- Technical planning and dependency mapping
- Escalation of cross-team design or platform concerns

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work with Developers to align implementation choices with the agreed architecture
- Collaborate with Product Managers to assess feasibility and sequencing of roadmap items
- Support Project Managers by clarifying technical dependencies, risks, and delivery constraints

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the testing strategy, validation approach, and release-readiness criteria. They ensure quality is built into delivery rather than treated as a final checkpoint.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation
- Coordinate functional, integration, and regression test planning
- Review release readiness and traceability to requirements
- Partner with Developers and Product Managers on acceptance criteria
- Escalate quality risks that could affect delivery or customer impact

### Goals
- Improve product quality and confidence before release
- Reduce defects escaping into production
- Align testing effort with business and customer risk

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and quality review meetings
- Defect triage and release readiness discussions
- Acceptance criteria clarifications with Product and engineering teams

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partner with Developers on testability, coverage, and bug triage
- Align with Product Managers on measurable acceptance standards and risk priorities
- Support Project Managers by validating whether milestones are actually ready to ship

---

## Release Managers / Delivery Coordinators

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate deployment activities, communications, and rollback planning so releases are predictable and safe.

### Responsibilities
- Manage release calendars, cutover windows, and readiness checks
- Coordinate go-live communication across stakeholders and support teams
- Prepare rollback and contingency plans for production changes
- Monitor release quality and post-deployment verification activities
- Partner with engineering and project leadership to handle release risk

### Goals
- Reduce deployment-related disruption
- Improve predictability and stakeholder confidence in releases
- Keep operational readiness aligned with delivery milestones

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and deployment dashboards
- Stakeholder announcements and incident coordination
- Post-release check-ins and go/no-go decisions

### How they interact with existing roles
- Work closely with Developers and QA Leads to confirm production readiness
- Coordinate with Project Managers on timelines and stakeholder comms
- Support Product Managers by aligning release timing to customer and business commitments

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners help ensure projects meet security requirements, governance expectations, and risk controls across the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks, security controls, and compliance needs
- Assess vulnerabilities, dependency risks, and sensitive data exposure
- Guide teams on secure design, access controls, and operational safeguards
- Support incident response planning and escalation processes
- Partner with leadership on risk acceptance and mitigation decisions

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk
- Build trust in project delivery and production operations
- Ensure teams move with appropriate controls and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and risk assessments
- Compliance check-ins and governance updates
- Escalations for high-risk findings or incidents

### How they interact with existing roles
- Partner with Developers to review secure implementation decisions and testing gaps
- Support Project Managers and Product Managers in understanding risk and approval requirements
- Collaborate with Release Managers to ensure release readiness includes security checks

---

## Customer Success / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Representatives provide direct feedback from users, customers, and operational teams, helping the project remain aligned to real-world needs.

### Responsibilities
- Share customer issues, feedback, and recurring pain points
- Help prioritize fixes and improvements based on service impact
- Validate whether a released feature is delivering value in practice
- Support stakeholder communication around product experience and adoption
- Identify training, rollout, or support needs tied to delivery changes

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce avoidable friction in product or service usage
- Inform product and delivery decisions with real operational evidence

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue escalation channels
- Support handoff and readiness discussions
- Post-release monitoring and customer impact updates

### How they interact with existing roles
- Share insights with Product Managers to validate demand and refine prioritization
- Coordinate with Project Managers on rollout messaging and customer communication
- Work with Developers and QA Leads to clarify defects, edge cases, and supportability concerns

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners help teams measure business outcomes, product health, and operational performance so decisions are evidence-based.

### Responsibilities
- Define key metrics, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Review product or service adoption, quality, and performance trends
- Support experiment and impact measurement across releases
- Partner with Product and Project leads on success metrics and retrospectives
- Flag changes that require measurement updates or operational follow-up

### Goals
- Make decisions using measurable outcomes rather than assumptions
- Improve visibility into product and delivery performance
- Support continuous improvement and learning cycles

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews and dashboard check-ins
- Metrics planning for milestones and releases
- Retrospective discussions on business outcomes

### How they interact with existing roles
- Help Product Managers define and monitor success metrics
- Support Project Managers with status reporting beyond task completion
- Collaborate with Developers and QA Leads to validate whether product changes improve user experience and operational health

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a more complete view of delivery ownership, accountability, and cross-functional collaboration.
